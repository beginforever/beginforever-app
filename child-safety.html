Last updated: May 2026

Our Commitment

Begin Forever is a matrimony platform strictly for adults aged 18 and above. We are fully committed to child safety and have zero tolerance for any content or behaviour that exploits or harms minors.

Age Verification

All users must confirm they are at least 18 years old during registration. Every profile is manually reviewed by our team and verified against a government-issued photo ID before being approved. Any profile found to belong to a minor is immediately removed and reported to the relevant authorities.

Prevention of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Begin Forever has a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We actively:

  • Require government ID verification for all users before profile approval
  • Manually review every profile before it becomes visible to other users
  • Provide in-app reporting tools for users to report suspicious or harmful content
  • Immediately suspend and investigate any account reported for child safety concerns
  • Report confirmed CSAM or CSAE to the relevant national authorities including the Cyber Crime Cell of India

In-App Reporting

Users can report any profile or content that raises child safety concerns directly within the app using the Report button available on every profile. All reports are reviewed by our team within 24 hours.

Compliance

Begin Forever complies with all applicable child safety laws in India including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act and the Information Technology Act. We report any confirmed cases of child sexual abuse material to the appropriate regional and national authorities.
